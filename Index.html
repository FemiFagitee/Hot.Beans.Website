About Us

Building Tomorrow's Web, Today

Hot Beans Web was formed in 2014 by brothers Mark and Stewart from the modest surroundings of their West Bridgford family home. After graduating in Computer Science from Leicester University in 2013, the brothers began building websites for family and friends — and quickly grew from there.

Now based in a modern office in the centre of Nottingham, the company has grown to more than 100 associates. Our mission is to provide a collaborative and exciting workplace that allows our developers to shine.

We are an award-winning web design agency working with some of the best small-to-medium businesses in the Midlands.

Junior Web Developer — Job Description

Junior web developers at Hot Beans Web assist the team in all aspects of website and application design. From day one you'll work on real client projects guided by a senior mentor.

  • Assisting with the plan and design process of a website
  • Assisting with the build and structure of a website
  • Writing and editing website content
  • Writing code in one or more programming or scripting languages
  • Implementing new design features and converting files to web formats
  • Assisting with testing and reviewing front and back-end applications
  • Maintaining webpages and performing updates
  • Keeping up to date with latest technology and programming trends

Skills Required

A solid understanding of website design and development principles is essential.

  • Proficient in HTML and CSS; good JavaScript knowledge
  • Competent in functional and unit-level testing
  • Excellent design skills with attention to detail
  • Understanding of responsive design principles
  • Knowledge of back-end: servers, APIs, SQL, databases
  • Understanding of Search Engine Optimisation
  • Strong analytical and communication skills
  • Ability to work under pressure to tight deadlines

Qualifications & Work Experience

A level 5 (HND) or level 6 (Degree) qualification in Web Development or a related field is essential. Any other relevant courses, certification or experience is desirable.

Accepted Qualifications:

  • Degree in Computer Science or Web Development (BSc Hons)
  • HND / HNC in IT or Computing (Level 5)
  • Recognised coding bootcamp completion
  • Open University Diploma in Computing, IT and Design

Experience:

  • 3-4 years professional or personal development experience
  • Portfolio of personal or academic projects
  • Open source contributions
  • Any freelance or voluntary web work

Career changers are very welcome! Like Steve Billings, who completed his qualification while working full time.
